# Site strategy

## Commercial purpose

Help a Tri-Cities visitor understand the two documented maintenance categories and start a quote conversation with minimal friction. Primary action: Request a Free Quote. Secondary action: Call Earth Effects.

This is a sales-presentation concept, not an authorized production website. Current operating status is unresolved because municipal records document an asset and contract transfer in 2022. Prospect qualification must precede any official launch.

## Audience and information hierarchy

The brief suggests local people who need property upkeep. Current property eligibility is unknown; the page does not assert a residential, commercial, municipal or industrial specialization.

1. Hero: clear lawn/landscape maintenance positioning, Unicoi/Tri-Cities context, two direct actions.
2. Orientation strip: work, area and first step in one glance.
3. Services: two substantial editorial rows, each preselecting its form option.
4. Local roots: listed location/service region and direct contact. Coverage is explicitly confirmed per property.
5. Process: tell us the need, start a conversation, confirm scope and availability. No company workflow promises.
6. Quote request: five requested fields with only phone optional, review step and email/copy handoff.
7. Contact and final CTA: complete published contact details, then one final conversion opportunity.

No real-work gallery or review block is fabricated. The practical trust case is transparent service scope, matching public contact details, locality, a readable site, and an honest contact flow. It is intentionally not a claim of proven quality or a substituted testimonial section.

## Conversion decisions

Repeated quote anchors lead to one form. Service links set the service field. The phone action stays visible in the header; a mobile bottom bar provides 58px call and quote targets. The short form asks for town/location in the project description instead of adding unrequested fields. The review shows exactly what will go into the draft. Editing preserves all details. Copy is a real fallback for absent mailto handlers.

No account, false backend or analytics is introduced. Measure conversion only after owner approval and an actual production lead channel are configured. Do not claim conversion gains without data.

## SEO and publication

A descriptive title, metadata, semantic headings, Open Graph text, favicon and WebPage JSON-LD are included. LocalBusiness JSON-LD is deliberately deferred until current operations are confirmed. No fabricated rating or owner authorization is encoded. noindex, nofollow and the independent-concept footer identify the preview. GitHub Pages serves relative-path assets on main without a framework or build step.

## Next factual improvements

First validate current operation and ownership. Then obtain authentic job photography, confirmed service details, property types, coverage, appointment rules and original reviews. Add only owner-approved facts and assets. A production form requires a real delivery/storage service, consent/privacy wording matching its actual handling, and monitored recipient confirmation.
