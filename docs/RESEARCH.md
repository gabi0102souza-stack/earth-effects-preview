## INFERENCE

- A quote-first website is a plausible commercial improvement, based on directory-only discoverability and the brief. No conversion baseline or business traffic data is available.
- Homeowners and property managers are plausible audiences. Current residential/commercial client mix is unconfirmed, so the public page uses neutral property wording.
- Local landscape photography can communicate place without pretending to show work. It does not prove coverage in Erwin or any additional town.
- An asset/contract sale does not, on its own, prove business closure or present ownership.
