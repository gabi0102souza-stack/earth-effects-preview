# Creative direction

Visual thesis: the plain-spoken strength of a local outdoor-service business, with place expressed by an authentic East Tennessee landscape and a large, practical headline.

## Design system

- Ink #202a24 and deep #1e2822 anchor the identity.
- Work-orange #f08b49 makes the conversion actions easy to see.
- Near-white #f7f6f2 supports clear type; a restrained pale gray-green #e7e9df distinguishes the form.
- Barlow Condensed Bold supplies compact, assertive headlines. DM Sans supplies readable supporting text and controls. Both are local WOFF2 assets under SIL OFL.
- Square corners, thin rules, broad editorial rows and deliberate open spacing avoid card grids and application-dashboard styling.
- Numbered section labels support scanning without decorative landscaping icons.
- The EE monogram is conceptual branding, not a copied or claimed official logo.

## Composition

Desktop uses a large typographic left half and regional photography on the right. One image carries the local atmosphere, without filler scenes or a fictitious project gallery. The actual provenance is visible in the caption. Services occupy two horizontal rows, followed by a contrasting locality panel. Contact information and the quote form are intentional visual destinations.

Mobile puts the proposition and quote action ahead of the photograph. Typography scales within the viewport, services become compact vertical rows, the form stacks, and a bottom bar keeps calling and requesting a quote within reach. The 320px layout stacks name/phone to protect input width. Footer spacing accommodates the fixed actions.

## Photography decision

No accessible asset was verifiably an Earth Effects project. The single photograph is Mark Fickett's CC BY 3.0 image of the Nolichucky River near Erwin. The photograph has a different role from a portfolio: place, not proof of work. It is cropped deliberately and labeled as regional scenery rather than company work.

## Copy and motion

American English, concrete service terms, no em dash, no grand claims, no made-up specialization or review. No decorative animation; smooth anchor scrolling respects reduced-motion preferences. The quote disclosure tells visitors exactly what the static site can do.
